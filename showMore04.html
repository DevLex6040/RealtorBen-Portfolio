The Full Brief

I provide professional assistance with the documentation required for land acquisition and property transactions in Nigeria. From reviewing and preparing essential documents such as Deeds of Assignment, Survey Plans, Governor's Consent, and Certificates of Occupancy (C of O), to ensuring compliance with legal requirements, I help clients navigate the complexities of property documentation. My services are designed to streamline the process, minimize risks, and ensure that all necessary paperwork is accurate and complete. Whether you are buying or selling property, my expertise in documentation will provide you with peace of mind and confidence in your transactions. I give you guidance through documentation process, helping you avoid common mistakes and move forward with greater confidence. My goal is to make the documentation process as smooth and efficient as possible, allowing you to focus on your property investment with assurance that all legal requirements are met.
